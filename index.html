Why join Botathon

Botathon 2K26 is a one-day build sprint. Teams pick their own problem, ship something that runs, and present it to reviewers the same day. No prior hackathon experience needed — bring a problem you care about and a laptop.

Open innovation

One track, no fixed problem statement. Bring a problem you care about, from any domain, and build a working solution for it in a day.

THE BRIEF

Pick any real problem — on campus, in your city, in an industry you know — and build something that runs. There is no released problem statement to wait for; your first job is choosing a problem worth a day of work.

WHAT YOU MUST SHIP

A working demo, not slides. Any language, framework or hardware is allowed. Pre-existing boilerplate is fine, a pre-built project is not, and every line you submit must be one you can explain.

HOW IT IS JUDGED

Reviewers weigh the problem as heavily as the build: why it matters, who it helps, and how far you got in a day. State the problem in your first slide and show the working solution in the rest.
